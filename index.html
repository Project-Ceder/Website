Rooted in the Cederberg

The Clanwilliam cedar — Widdringtonia cedarbergensis — is an endangered tree endemic to South Africa's Cederberg mountains. Resilient, slow-growing, and deeply rooted, it has weathered centuries of fire and drought.

Project Ceder takes its name from this tree. We believe conservation deserves the same calibre of technology leadership found in the private sector — patient, grounded, and built to endure.
